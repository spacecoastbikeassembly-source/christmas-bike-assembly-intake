4. Stage A — Customer pickup: BOX ONLY

Final payment — record when collected

Receipt IDs are internal references generated in your browser, not bank/card processor transaction IDs. For electronic payments, enter the actual processor confirmation separately. Keep the editable backup to preserve both receipt IDs.

Deposit request formula: $50 standard or $75 e-bike + $25 first storage period if selected + $50 for requested/confirmed Christmas Eve delivery + 50% of the additional mileage fee. Mileage is computed as $2 × driving miles beyond 10 from the workshop; verify the route and get customer approval before collecting payment. Do not mark a payment received until collected. The balance is the total minus other prior payments, the deposit, and the final payment. A requested Christmas Eve delivery adds $50 to the provisional quote and deposit request; it is NOT a confirmed delivery reservation until you change its status to Confirmed with customer.

8. E-bike battery custody and storage

Only e-bikes with removable batteries qualify for storage. Batteries and chargers must be returned promptly to the customer after setup/testing; do not store them overnight.
